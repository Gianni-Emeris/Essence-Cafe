Essence Café is a small local restaurant located by the Square, 4 Chatterton Road in Pietermaritzburg. Essence Café have been operating since 2017. Essence Café has a wide menu offering different breakfasts, burgers, pizzas, sandwich, salads, fish, seafood, drinks and much more. They also have vegetarian friendly, vegan options and gluten options. Essence Café is your go to for delicious moments. A cozy spot with incredible food crafted from local goodness. Its not just a meal, its an experience. Relax in a stylish setting where good taste meets comfort. The target audience is anyone that wants delicious food with a cosy experience.

Owner Martin Maltby named the restaurant "Essence" because he believes the word encompasses the "soul" of the business: “Good food, good friends and excellence,” and he certainly has devised a comprehensive menu that caters for just about everyone.

Essence Cafe is open from Tuessday's to Friday from 7:30 AM to 9:00 PM, Friday's from 7:30 AM to 9:00 PM and Sunday's from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
